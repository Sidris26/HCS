Hospital Information Systems (HIS): Computer systems used to manage information throughout a hospital, including patient, clinical, financial, and administrative information.

Laboratory Information Systems (LIS): Systems used by laboratories to manage patient test orders, laboratory results, and other laboratory information.

eHealth Exchange: A network that allows healthcare organizations to securely exchange electronic health information between different systems and organizations.

Nursing Information Systems (NIS): Systems designed to support nursing care through documentation, patient information, charting, and staff scheduling.

Picture Archiving and Communication Systems (PACS): Systems used to store, retrieve, and view medical images such as X-rays, CT scans, and MRIs.

Pharmacy Information Systems (PIS): Systems used to manage medication information, prescriptions, dispensing, and pharmacy records.

Radiology Information Systems (RIS): Systems used to manage radiology scheduling, examination tracking, and reporting.

Interoperability: The ability of different healthcare computer systems to communicate, exchange information, and use the information they receive.

EMR vs. EHR: An EMR is generally maintained by one healthcare organization. An EHR is broader and can contain health information from multiple providers and care settings. EMR information can contribute to a patient's broader EHR.
